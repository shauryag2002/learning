# 🚀 Welcome to Android Engineering: The Mentorship Roadmap

Hey there! Welcome to the team! 👋 

I'm your senior Android developer buddy for this journey. You’re coming into Android at arguably the **best time in its history**. A few years ago, Android development was messy: tons of boilerplate Java, finicky XML layouts, `findViewById` crashes at runtime, and fragmented lifecycles. 

Today? Android is sleek, expressive, and fun. We have **Kotlin**, **Jetpack Compose** (declarative UI), **Coroutines** (effortless async logic), and modern architectural patterns.

Here is how we are breaking down our learning into digestible, bite-sized chapters. No overwhelming firehoses—just clear intuition, practical "why" behind every decision, and industry-standard practices.

## 💡 How We'll Learn

1. **The "Why" First:** We won't just tell you "click this button". You will learn why engineering teams do it that way.
2. **Mental Models Over Memorization:** Mobile engineering is about lifecycles, states, and resource constraints. Once you grasp the mental model, syntax is easy.
3. **No Bad Questions:** If anything feels confusing, speak up! That's what a mentor is here for.
